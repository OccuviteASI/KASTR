# KASTR Requirements

Every requirement below traces to a request made during development, in the
requester's intent if not verbatim. Status is against **v0.7.0**.

Legend: ✅ implemented · 🚫 removed at the requester's direction · ⏸ deferred.
"Version" is where the requirement (or its final form) landed; blank means it
predates versioning (v0.5, 2026-08-25).
