Download certificate

One-time setup on the phone

  1. Open the link (scan the code) once over http or download the certificate here and send it to the phone.
  2. iPhone: Settings › Profile Downloaded › Install, then Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings › turn on KASTR Local CA.
  3. Android: Settings › Security › More security › Install a certificate › CA certificate › pick kastr-ca.crt.
  4. Then open the https link above: the phone joins with camera and microphone like any other KASTR.
